# The 64 new tools in Toolbox 0.2

All entries link to their local route. See the README for supported formats and limitations.

## Image Tools

- **[Rotate & Flip Image](/tools/image-rotate-flip)** — A fresh perspective, in 90-degree steps.
- **[Image to Grayscale](/tools/image-grayscale)** — Strip back the color. Keep the character.
- **[Image Brightness](/tools/image-brightness)** — A little lighter. A little more balanced.
- **[Image Watermark](/tools/image-watermark)** — Add your name, credit, or copyright to a photo.
- **[Image Collage](/tools/image-collage)** — Bring your favorite images into one neat grid.

## PDF & Document Tools

- **[Word to PDF](/tools/word-to-pdf)** — Your Word document. A beautifully portable PDF. Supports .docx, not legacy .doc. Browser-rendered pages preserve common formatting and images; complex layouts, fonts, fields and tracked changes can differ from Microsoft Word. Output is image-based, not searchable.
- **[Combine Word to PDF](/tools/combine-word-to-pdf)** — Many Word documents. One perfectly ordered PDF. Add multiple .docx files, arrange their order, and export one PDF. Complex Word layouts may differ. PDF pages are image-based.
- **[Mixed Files to PDF](/tools/documents-to-pdf)** — Bring Word, PDFs, and images together in one file. Mix .docx, PDF, PNG, JPG and WebP. Existing PDF pages stay searchable; Word and image pages are rendered as images.
- **[Word to Text](/tools/word-to-text)** — Extract the words. Leave the formatting behind. Extracts paragraph text from .docx files. Images, headers and advanced formatting are not included.
- **[Text to Word](/tools/text-to-word)** — Turn your notes into a ready-to-share Word document.
- **[Text to PDF](/tools/text-to-pdf)** — Give plain text a clean, printable home. Text is rendered with browser fonts into image-based A4 PDF pages.
- **[Markdown to PDF](/tools/markdown-to-pdf)** — From simple Markdown to a polished PDF. Supports headings, lists, code, links and emphasis. HTML is sanitized. Output is image-based.
- **[PDF Text to Word](/tools/pdf-to-word)** — Move selectable PDF text into an editable Word file. Text-only extraction, not a layout-preserving converter. Scanned documents require OCR, which is not included.
- **[Watermark PDF](/tools/pdf-watermark)** — Make it yours with a subtle text watermark.
- **[Add PDF Page Numbers](/tools/pdf-page-numbers)** — Keep every page in the picture, and in order.
- **[PDF Metadata Editor](/tools/pdf-metadata-editor)** — Set the title, author, subject, and keywords.
- **[PDF to Text](/tools/pdf-to-text)** — Take selectable text out of your PDF. Works with selectable text. Scanned image-only PDFs need OCR, which is not included.
- **[Resize PDF Pages](/tools/pdf-resize)** — Fit every page to A4, Letter, or A3.
- **[Reverse PDF Pages](/tools/pdf-reverse)** — Last page first. Reverse your entire document.
- **[Duplicate PDF Pages](/tools/pdf-duplicate)** — Repeat your document pages in a single PDF.

## Text Tools

- **[Remove Duplicate Lines](/tools/remove-duplicate-lines)** — Keep the first occurrence. Skip the repetition.
- **[Email Extractor](/tools/extract-emails)** — Find unique email addresses in a block of text.
- **[URL Extractor](/tools/extract-urls)** — Pull HTTP and HTTPS links out of your text.
- **[URL Slug Generator](/tools/slug-generator)** — Turn a title into a clean, link-friendly slug.
- **[Reverse Text](/tools/reverse-text)** — Flip your words around, one character at a time.
- **[Remove Whitespace](/tools/remove-whitespace)** — Clear spaces, tabs, and line breaks in one click.
- **[Text Repeater](/tools/text-repeater)** — Repeat a little. Or a lot. You choose.
- **[Find & Replace](/tools/find-replace)** — Find exact matches and replace them everywhere.
- **[Text to Binary](/tools/text-to-binary)** — Encode your text as UTF-8 binary bytes.
- **[Binary to Text](/tools/binary-to-text)** — Make readable text from a sequence of binary bytes.
- **[HTML Entity Encoder](/tools/html-entity-encoder)** — Safely escape special characters for HTML.
- **[HTML Entity Decoder](/tools/html-entity-decoder)** — Turn HTML entities back into readable text.

## Developer Tools

- **[JSON to YAML](/tools/json-to-yaml)** — The same data, in a more human-friendly format.
- **[YAML to JSON](/tools/yaml-to-json)** — Turn YAML into validated, formatted JSON.
- **[XML to JSON](/tools/xml-to-json)** — Convert structured XML into readable JSON.
- **[JSON to XML](/tools/json-to-xml)** — Give your JSON data an XML structure.
- **[JWT Decoder](/tools/jwt-decoder)** — Inspect token headers and payloads locally. Decoding does not verify signatures or establish token trust. Never treat decoded claims as authenticated.

- **[JavaScript Minifier](/tools/javascript-minifier)** — Make your JavaScript lighter with Terser.

- **[JSON Minifier](/tools/json-minifier)** — Remove the whitespace, keep every value.
- **[JSON Key Sorter](/tools/json-key-sorter)** — Sort object keys, all the way down.
- **[Number Base Converter](/tools/number-base-converter)** — Binary, octal, decimal, or hex. Your number, your way.
- **[HMAC Generator](/tools/hmac-generator)** — Create a keyed SHA-256 signature, privately.

## Calculator & Converter Tools

- **[BMI Calculator](/tools/bmi-calculator)** — A quick body mass index calculation. For adults. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis or personalized health advice.
- **[Loan Calculator](/tools/loan-calculator)** — See the monthly payment before you commit. Estimates fixed-rate amortizing loans. Excludes fees, taxes, insurance and lender-specific rounding.
- **[Compound Interest](/tools/compound-interest)** — See how your savings could grow over time. Assumes monthly compounding, constant rates and end-of-month contributions. Not financial advice.
- **[Simple Interest](/tools/simple-interest)** — Calculate interest without the compounding.
- **[Tip & Bill Splitter](/tools/tip-calculator)** — A fair split. A thoughtful tip. No mental math.
- **[Sales Tax Calculator](/tools/sales-tax-calculator)** — Know the tax, and the total, before checkout.
- **[Date Difference](/tools/date-difference)** — Count the days between any two dates.
- **[Aspect Ratio Calculator](/tools/aspect-ratio-calculator)** — Find the right proportions for your next frame.
- **[Average Calculator](/tools/average-calculator)** — Mean, median, and range. Make sense of your numbers.
- **[Fraction Calculator](/tools/fraction-calculator)** — Add two fractions and simplify the answer.
- **[Running Pace Calculator](/tools/pace-calculator)** — Find your minutes per kilometer and average speed.
- **[Fuel Cost Calculator](/tools/fuel-cost-calculator)** — Plan the drive. Know the fuel budget.

## Business Tools

- **[CSV to Excel](/tools/csv-to-excel)** — Make a real Excel workbook from your CSV.
- **[Excel to CSV](/tools/excel-to-csv)** — A spreadsheet, simplified. Export a sheet as CSV.
- **[Excel to JSON](/tools/excel-to-json)** — Turn spreadsheet rows into structured JSON.
- **[JSON to Excel](/tools/json-to-excel)** — From structured data to an organized workbook.
- **[Spreadsheet Viewer](/tools/spreadsheet-viewer)** — Open an Excel workbook without installing Excel. Reads .xlsx files and lets you select a worksheet. Previews the first 100 rows; exports include every row. Formulas display cached values, not recalculated results.

## Utility Tools

- **[Create ZIP](/tools/zip-creator)** — A whole collection. One tidy ZIP file.
- **[Extract ZIP](/tools/zip-extractor)** — See what’s inside. Download just what you need. ZIP only. Password-protected and encrypted archives are not supported. Archives are limited to 50 MB input and 200 MB expanded data.
- **[File Checksum](/tools/file-hash)** — Check file integrity with a SHA-256 fingerprint.
